# Fountain of Dreams (1990) Strategy Guide

## Scope and spoiler policy

This guide covers the Electronic Arts 1990 RPG **Fountain of Dreams**, the spiritual (though not official) successor to *Wasteland*. It is intended for new players exploring post-apocalyptic Florida after "The Change." This guide contains mechanical information and location spoilers.

---

## Overview and Objective

*Fountain of Dreams* takes place in a mutated, isolated Florida. Society has fractured into factions following a global catastrophe and a localized event known as "The Change." Your goal is to lead a party of three characters to uncover the secrets of the mutations, survive the gang wars, and eventually locate the legendary Fountain of Dreams.

The game uses an engine near-identical to *Wasteland*, meaning tactical movement, skill checks, and brutal combat are the core pillars of the experience.

---

## The Party

A party in *Fountain of Dreams* consists of up to **three characters**. You can choose from five distinct professions during creation. Unlike *Wasteland*, there is no separate NPC recruitment for the core party slots; your initial three are your only primary combatants.

### Professions

| Profession | Focus | Starting Skills | CON |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|
| **Survivalist** | Balanced | Pharmacy, Mechanics, Stealth, Handgun, Gunsmith | 20-25 |
| **Vigilante** | Combat | Demolition, Brawling, Handgun, Gunsmith | 20-25 |
| **Medic** | Healing | Medic (L2), Perception, Blades | 15-20 |
| **Hood** | Stealth | Lockpick (L2), Stealth, Handgun | 15-25 |
| **Mechanic** | Technical | Bomb/Alarm Disarm, Mechanic, Demolition, Brawling | 15-25 |

**Recommendation:** A balanced party usually includes a **Survivalist** or **Vigilante** for front-line combat, a **Medic** for survival, and a **Mechanic** or **Hood** for technical obstacles.

---

## Attributes

Characters have seven core attributes, ranging from 3 to 20.

- **ST (Strength):** Physical power and carrying capacity.
- **IQ (Intelligence):** Critical for **Active** skills. Every point above 15 grants a bonus to active skill levels.
- **DX (Dexterity):** Accuracy in combat and speed.
- **WP (Willpower):** Mental resilience and resistance to some mutations.
- **AP (Appeal/Perception):** Critical for **Passive** skills. Every point above 15 grants a bonus to passive skill levels.
- **CH (Charisma):** Affects interactions with NPCs and shop prices.
- **LK (Luck):** A general modifier for critical hits and avoiding traps.

---

## Skills Reference

Skills are divided into **Active** (concentrated effort) and **Passive** (automatic checks).

### Active Skills (IQ-based)
- **Medic:** Essential for stopping bleeding and stabilizing seriously wounded allies.
- **Lockpick:** Used to bypass doors and chests.
- **Climb:** Required for vertical navigation.
- **Pharmacy:** Identifying drugs and treating poisons.
- **Bomb/Alarm Disarm:** Essential for survival in trap-heavy dungeons.
- **Mechanic / Electronics:** Used to repair pre-Change relics.
- **Doctor:** Required for advanced ailments like radiation and rabies.
- **Brawling / Handgun:** The primary skills for unarmed and pistol combat.

### Passive Skills (AP-based)
- **Gunsmith:** Automatically helps unjam weapons in combat.
- **Perception:** Spots hidden items and traps as you walk.
- **Stealth:** Reduces the frequency of random encounters.
- **Language:** Allows understanding of diverse faction dialects.
- **Demolitions:** Knowledge used when handling explosives.
- **Blades:** Proficiency with knives and swords.

## Combat and Survival

### Health and Condition
Health is measured in **CON** (Constitution).
- **Lightly Wounded:** CON is reduced but the character functions normally.
- **Unconscious (UNC):** CON below 1. The character is incapacitated but may recover.
- **Serious (SER) / Critical (CRT) / Mortal (MOR):** Worsening states that lead to death without immediate medical intervention.
- **Comatose (COM):** The final stage before death.
- **Dead (DED):** Permanent loss.

### Mutations
"The Change" has left Florida's inhabitants prone to mutations. Your characters can be **Afflicted** or **Unafflicted**. Pay attention to messages like *"Mutations are acting up!"* as they can grant powerful boons or debilitating penalties.

### Healing Services
Natural healing is slow. For serious injuries or training, visit medical centers:
- **Doc Marino's**
- **Doc Brewhoe's**
- **DeMedici's**

These locations offer healing, poison cures, and skill training for a price.

## Exploration Tips

1. **Save Frequently:** *Fountain of Dreams* is notoriously difficult. A single bad combat round or a failed trap disarm can wipe a 3-person party.
2. **Manage Ammo:** Early game ammunition is scarce. Use **Brawling** or **Blades** for weak enemies to conserve your `.45 clips` and `9mm` magazines.
3. **Use Perception:** Always have your character with the highest AP (and Perception skill) lead the party.
4. **Stabilize First:** In combat, if a Ranger goes down, prioritize using the **Medic** skill immediately. A "Serious" wound will quickly become "Dead" if left untreated for even a few rounds.
5. **Check the Map:** The "post-Change" Florida landscape is treacherous. Use the Map tab in your trainer to track your coordinates and avoid wandering into high-level gang territory too early.
