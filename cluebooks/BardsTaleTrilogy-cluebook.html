BT1 — Tales of the Unknown

Skara Brae has fallen under the spell of Mangar the Dark. Build a party, explore the city and Mangar's Tower, and confront the wizard at its summit.

BT2 — The Destiny Knight

Travel the realm of Tangramayne to recover the Destiny Stone. The journey spans wilderness, cities, dungeons, and the seven realms of the underworld.

BT3 — Thief of Fate

Tarjan has stolen the Destiny Wand and shattered time itself. Pursue him through the realms of time, collect the tools to oppose him, and restore the world.

The trainer's Maps tab provides the complete area catalogue and supports in-game navigation. This cluebook focuses on durable reference material and progression guidance.

Classes and races

Races

Human, Elf, Dwarf, Hobbit, Half-Elf, Half-Orc, and Gnome are available throughout the remaster. Select a race that supports the class and party role you want to develop.

Classes

ClassRoleFirst gameNotes
WarriorFighterBT1The basic fighter, able to use nearly every weapon in the game. Gains an extra attack for every 4 levels after the 1st.
PaladinFighterBT1A holy warrior. Gains multiple attacks like a Warrior, wields weapons others cannot, and has greatly increased resistance to evil magic.
RogueStealthBT1A professional thief with moderate combat ability. Hides in shadows, identifies items, and searches for and disarms traps — the only safe way to open a trapped chest.
BardHybridBT1A warrior who turned to music. Uses most warrior weapons and plays magical tunes, one per experience level before his throat runs dry. Needs an instrument equipped.
HunterFighterBT1An assassin who strikes at vital areas. Has a chance — growing with experience — to score a critical hit and kill a foe outright.
MonkFighterBT1A martial artist. Can use weapons but does far better without them at higher levels, and his armour class improves with every level gained.
ConjurerCasterBT1Creates and manipulates objects and light. One of the two magical schools open at character creation.
MagicianCasterBT1Bestows effects on ordinary objects, increasing their capability or changing their form. The other school open at character creation.
SorcererCasterBT1Illusion and perception. Not available at creation — a magic user must first have learned spell level 3 in one other school.
WizardCasterBT1Summons and binds supernatural creatures. Not available at creation — a magic user must first have learned spell level 3 in two other schools.
ArchmageCasterBT2The master of all four basic schools. The promotion itself can only be taken in BT2, even by a character who met the requirements in BT1.
ChronomancerCasterBT3Bends time itself. Requires mastery of three magical schools — and gives up every spell learned in them on promotion.
GeomancerCasterBT3The fighter's road into magic, drawing power from the earth. Open only to fighting classes, and only once unlocked during BT3's story.

Walkthrough

BT1 — Skara Brae and Mangar's Tower

  1. Create a balanced group with front-line fighters, a Rogue, a Bard, and at least two spellcasters.
  2. Explore Skara Brae methodically, buy equipment from Garth, and use the Review Board and inn between expeditions.
  3. Learn the tower's routes, conserve spell points for dangerous encounters, and climb Mangar's Tower only after the party can recover from attrition.
  4. Defeat Mangar at the top of the tower to free Skara Brae.

BT2 — The Destiny Stone

  1. Carry forward a seasoned BT1 party or establish a new balanced party in Tangramayne.
  2. Use wilderness towns as safe resupply points while collecting the clues and artifacts that open the underworld realms.
  3. Recruit an Archmage once the four basic magic schools have been developed; the promotion is available only in BT2.
  4. Recover the Destiny Stone and complete the final confrontation to secure Tangramayne.

BT3 — Thief of Fate

  1. Begin with a capable mixed party and keep a reliable supply of healing, light, detection, and escape magic.
  2. Use time-travel destinations to collect the tools and knowledge required to pursue Tarjan.
  3. Chronomancers and Geomancers become available in this chapter; plan class changes around their different promotion paths.
  4. Face Tarjan only after the party has the required quest tools and enough reserves to survive a long final sequence.

Strategy notes

  • Build for roles. Keep durable fighters in front, bring a Rogue for traps, and carry enough magic to heal, light, detect, and escape.
  • Map every area. Use the trainer's Maps tab with the game's own map data to keep track of cities, wilderness, and dungeon routes.
  • Manage spell progression. School levels grant spells in bulk. Record which schools a character has left: the Review Board does not permit returning to a departed magic school.
  • Use ZZGO carefully. The Dream Spell is a BT2 travel tool. Its destination must be valid for the chapter currently loaded by the game.
  • Save before commitments. Class changes, deep-dungeon expeditions, and major quest hand-ins are all safer with a current save.
  • Protect vital items. Identify and preserve quest items. A party with strong equipment but without a required plot item can still be blocked.
  • Treat NUKE as a reserve. Gotterdammerung is exceptionally destructive; keep it for encounters where ending the fight immediately is worth the cost.
